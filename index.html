What we serve

From your morning coffee to a late lunch

Coffee

Strong coffee with a wide choice of syrups to add. Oat and soya milk are available for coffees and teas.

Café lunches

Sandwiches, paninis, bagels and jacket potatoes for a lunchtime bite.

Cakes & bakes

Cookies, warm cookie pies and a rotating selection of cakes and pies, so there is often something new on the counter.

Vegan options

Vegan choices across the menu, from cakes and cookie pies to jacket potatoes with vegan cheese.

About

A local coffee shop in Todmorden

Amicis is at 22 Halifax Road in Todmorden. Pop in for a strong coffee with your choice of syrup, grab some lunch, or treat yourself to something sweet from the cake counter.

The coffee shop is run by Bar Amicis Ltd, a company incorporated in August 2020 and run by Hayley Davies.
